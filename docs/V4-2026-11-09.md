## Three independent systems

1. Hero cloud: short-lived GPU density, visual only.
2. Temporary painting: sampled from actual visible ink alpha; each cell stores its own `holdUntil` at the moment of repaint.
3. Permanent painting: shared final Drops alpha; no automatic expiry, but NPCs can erode it.

Temporary hold is piecewise linear through `(radius, seconds) = (18,28), (90,38), (170,48)`, clamped to 28–48 seconds, followed by the existing five-second fade. Later growth never changes older deadlines; actual contact repaints and refreshes only touched cells. NPC erosion modifies both layers and preserves the no-rebound guard. The max-alpha union remains the displayed progress and uses the unchanged 50% success threshold.
